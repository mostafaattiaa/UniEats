# Uni Eats - Phase 1 (Team 4)

Our Phase 1 presentation for the mobile app project. Uni Eats is an app for university students: you pick your university, choose a food outlet, order, and pick a time to collect your food, so you don't have to stand in the queue between lectures.

We also looked at two other ideas (Study Spot and GymMind AI) and compared them before picking Uni Eats.

## The slides

- Web version: https://unieats-six.vercel.app
- PowerPoint: [UniEats_Phase1.pptx](UniEats_Phase1.pptx)

In the web version use the arrow keys (or the buttons) to change slides, and press F for full screen. If you download the repo and open `index.html`, keep the `images` folder next to it or the pictures won't show.

The slides cover the team, the problem, our survey video, the three ideas, the gap comparison, the decision matrix, a SWOT analysis, the app screens, how the app works and the features. The app screens are concept designs, not a finished app.
