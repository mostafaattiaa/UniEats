# UniEats: Skip the Queue. Enjoy Your Break.

A multi-university food ordering and scheduled pickup app. Students choose their university, order from campus food outlets and pick a collection time, so they don't spend their break in a queue.

**Phase 1 presentation:** Computers in Practice / Creative and Technological Industries project, Mobile Programming.
