Target users & operating model

Two sides. One flow.

🎓 Student user

Chooses a university→Browses outlets→Adds to cart→Picks a pickup slot→Gets confirmation

👨‍🍳 Restaurant user

Receives new orders→Confirms or rejects→Prepares→Marks ready for pickup
🏫

Multi-university model: every food outlet is linked to one university. Students pick their university first, and the app filters restaurants and menus to match.

⚠️

Key assumption: each restaurant controls its own pickup slots, so the app only shows times the outlet can really prepare and hand over the order.

Risks & considerations

What could go wrong, and our answer

Restaurant can't meet the pickup time
→
Outlets manage capacity and publish their own pickup slots.
Wrong menu or price information
→
Staff can update menus and mark items unavailable.
Student picks the wrong university
→
Show the selected university clearly and let users change it.
Late order preparation
→
Realistic preparation buffers, and outlets update order status.
Low adoption by restaurants
→
Start with a small pilot of one or two outlets, then expand.
Conclusion

Less waiting.
More break.

UniEats is a practical, student-centred answer to long campus queues. Its multi-university structure leaves room to grow, while a focused MVP keeps the first version achievable this semester.

🎯 Success = a student orders, then collects at the chosen time with less waiting and without missing their next lecture.
